Apple Cinnamon Rolls

Ingredients

    Dough:
  • 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons warm milk (90 degrees F/32 degrees C)
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 1/3 cup white sugar
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
    • 1 large egg, beaten 3/4 teaspoon fine salt 4 1/4 cups all purpose flour Filling: 4 tablespoons soft butter 1/3 cup apple butter 1/3 cup brown sugar 1/3 cup white sugar 2 tablespoons cinnamon 1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional) Bath: 4 cups apple cider 2/3 cup cream Icing: 1/4 cup reduced apple cider 2 cups powdered sugar
